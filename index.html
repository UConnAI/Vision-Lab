Recognize, then respond.

A pretrained Google MediaPipe Gesture Recognizer supplies hand landmarks and gesture labels. We map Closed_Fist → rock, Open_Palm → paper and Victory → scissors. A stable label locks your move; game rules choose the computer’s response.

Classic: the computer commits before SHOW. Counter move: it sees your recognized hand, then deliberately chooses the winning response. That mode demonstrates recognition and a rule—not early prediction.

Camera frames are processed locally. No video is saved or uploaded. Practice buttons demonstrate game behavior without testing camera recognition. The original lecture’s tiny CNN explains training; this game uses an already-trained model.
