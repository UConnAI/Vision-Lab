EXERCISE 1 / FILTER DETECTIVE

What does a filter notice?

Predict which filter will respond to a vertical boundary. Click pixels to change the image; click a response cell to inspect its exact calculation. Keyboard: Tab enters each grid; arrow keys move between cells, Home/End move across a row, and Space or Enter activates the cell.

8 × 8 pixels

3 × 3 filter weights

6 × 6 response

3 × 3 max pooling

The same nine weights slide across every 3 × 3 patch: multiply matching values, then add. We use stride 1 and no padding or bias. Pooling keeps the largest value in each 2 × 2 block. These filters are hand-designed; this page does not train a CNN.

Team mission

Draw a letter or symbol. Find a filter that highlights it. Turn ReLU off: what happened to the opposite edge? Blur the image: where did the fractional values come from? Explain why an edge detector alone cannot tell a cat from a hand.
