Input frames are centered in a padded square to preserve ball proportions during CNN resizing. Yellow outlines mark CNN positions; blue outlines mark appearance-tracked positions. Replacement size changes the artwork only. The CNN labels “sports ball”; it does not distinguish soccer from every other ball. Tracking begins at the largest detected ball, then prefers the nearest candidate. For a sufficiently colorful CNN-detected ball, an appearance tracker measures matching colored regions in the decoded frames when the CNN misses. It does not invent a CNN detection. If neither method supplies a position, the original stays visible. Between valid samples, positions are interpolated—those positions are not new CNN detections. Replacement is a 2D overlay, so it can cover a foot during occlusion.
